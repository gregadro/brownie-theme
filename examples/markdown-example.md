# Salted Caramel Brownies

> The best brownie is the one you are eating right now.

## Ingredients

- **12 tablespoons** unsalted butter, _melted_
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- ~~raisins~~ (never)
- 1 teaspoon `salt`

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to **180 °C**.
2. Mix everything, see [the recipe][tasty].
3. Bake for _25 minutes_.

```js
const brownie = bake({ minutes: 25 });
```

| Ingredient | Amount |
| ---------- | -----: |
| Butter     |  170 g |
| Sugar      |  200 g |
